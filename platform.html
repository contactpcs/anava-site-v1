Measure

EEG Diagnostics & Brain-Activity Reports

We record EEG in our clinics and process it through our own platform — turning raw brain activity into a structured report your clinician can actually use. Frequency-band analysis, hemispheric balance and session-over-session comparison are presented clearly, so a treatment decision rests on measured data rather than impression alone.

Clinicians get the depth: band power, asymmetry indices and trend data across a course of treatment. Patients get a plain-language version of the same report — what was recorded, what it indicates, and what changed since last time.
