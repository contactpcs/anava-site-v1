What the information on this website is — and what it is not.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

In an emergency, do not use this website.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, or you are having thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. In India you can also reach the Tele-MANAS national mental health helpline on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416, available 24 hours a day. A web form is not a route to urgent help and is not monitored around the clock.

1. General information only

All content on this website — including descriptions of conditions, treatments, neuromodulation modalities such as tDCS, tVNS, TMS and TPS, references to research, and any illustrative interface views of our software platform — is provided by ANAVA Clinics Private Limited for general information and educational purposes only.

It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a substitute for consultation with a qualified medical practitioner who has assessed you personally.

2. No doctor–patient relationship

Reading this website, submitting an enquiry, or registering your interest in a clinic does not create a doctor–patient relationship between you and ANAVA Clinics Private Limited, or between you and any clinician named on or associated with this website. Such a relationship is established only after a formal clinical assessment and the appropriate consent process.

3. Always seek professional advice

  • Consult a qualified medical practitioner before acting on anything you read here.
  • Never stop, start, reduce or change any prescribed medication on the basis of this website. Do so only under the supervision of the prescribing doctor. Abrupt discontinuation of psychiatric or neurological medication can be dangerous.
  • Do not delay seeking medical advice because of something you have read here.
  • Tell your treating doctor about any therapy you are considering, including neuromodulation.

4. Results differ between individuals

Neuromodulation is not effective for everyone, and no outcome can be promised. Where this website refers to published research, response rates or clinical evidence, those findings describe studied populations under study conditions. They are not a prediction of your own result.

Whether a therapy is suitable for you, and what benefit it might offer, depends on your diagnosis, your history, your medication, your other conditions and factors that can only be assessed in person. Some people experience no benefit. Some people are not suitable candidates at all.

5. Suitability and contraindications

Neuromodulation therapies have contraindications and precautions. Depending on the modality, these may include implanted electronic or metallic devices (such as a pacemaker, cochlear implant or deep brain stimulator), a history of seizures or epilepsy, certain skin or scalp conditions, pregnancy, and certain medications.

This is not a complete list, and this website cannot tell you whether a therapy is safe for you. Suitability is determined only through clinical screening by a qualified clinician, who will ask about your full history before any treatment is offered.

6. Clinical decisions rest with your clinician

ANAVA Clinics designs and builds its own software platform to support clinical work — recording intake, protocols, session parameters, safety checks and follow-up, and surfacing candidate protocols for a clinician to review. Every such suggestion is a proposal only.

All clinical decisions are made by your treating clinician. Our platform supports their judgement; it does not replace it, and it is not an autonomous diagnostic or prescribing system.

7. Devices and regulatory status

Neuromodulation devices are operated by trained clinical staff, to protocols set by a treating clinician. The regulatory approval status and the approved indications of any given device vary by jurisdiction and change over time. Nothing on this website should be read as a claim that a particular device is approved for a particular indication in your jurisdiction.

Their qualifications are as stated to us, and they practise under their own professional registration and responsibility. We are not responsible for clinical care they provide in their own independent practice.

We make no warranty that the content is complete, current or free from error, and we may update it at any time without notice.
