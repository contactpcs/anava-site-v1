Who we are

Built for patients who were told there was nothing more.

ANAVA Clinics Pvt Ltd delivers evidence-based, non-invasive neuromodulation — precisely targeted stimulation applied from outside the skull to the brain circuits involved in a condition. Nothing is implanted, there is no anaesthesia and no downtime. Patients return to their day straight after a session.

Therapies

What we offer today — and what's next.

Available now

tDCS

Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation

A gentle, low-amplitude current through scalp electrodes modulates excitability in specific brain regions. Sessions run 20–30 minutes.

Available now

tVNS

Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation

A small ear clip stimulates a branch of the vagus nerve to help regulate stress, mood and autonomic balance. Sessions run 20–60 minutes.

Available now

EEG diagnostics

Measure before we treat

Brain-activity recordings processed by our platform into clinician reports and plain-language summaries, repeated across a course of care.

The technology

One in-house platform behind every step of care.

Designed and built by our own technology team in Pune, the ANAVA platform connects the whole pathway.
