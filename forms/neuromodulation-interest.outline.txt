SECTION 3 of 5  · section break item 1859639582
  Title: "About neuromodulation"
  Description:
    | Neuromodulation uses very gentle stimulation to support the brain's natural ability to regulate mood, stress and sleep.
    | 
    | - tDCS: a soft headband with two damp sponge pads delivers a very mild current for about 20 - 30 minutes.
    | - tVNS: a small ear clip gently stimulates a nerve linked to calm and relaxation.
    | 
    | Both are non-invasive and work alongside your current therapy and medication. Most people feel only a mild tingling during the session. Sessions are guided by Prerna Shah and the Anava Clinics team.
    | 
    | Learn more: https://anavaclinics.com
