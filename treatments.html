Our therapies

Evidence-based neurotherapy,
delivered with precision

tDCS and tVNS are fully available in our clinics today, with TMS and TPS joining our offering soon, alongside EEG diagnostics that guide and track every course. Every modality is selected based on your individual clinical profile — all non-invasive, all supervised, all evidence-informed.

Safety & approach

What makes neurotherapy different

Unlike medication, which acts systemically through body chemistry, neurotherapy works directly at the level of neural circuits. It does not require anaesthesia, does not produce systemic side effects, and does not interact with most medications.

Non-invasive

No needles, no surgery, no anaesthesia required.

No downtime

Most patients return to work and daily life immediately after sessions.

Supervised

A qualified clinician is present throughout every session.

Evidence-based

Protocols drawn from peer-reviewed research and clinical evidence.

Brain stimulation

tDCS — Transcranial
Direct Current Stimulation

A gentle, low-amplitude electrical current — smaller than you would feel from a standard battery — is delivered through electrodes placed on the scalp. This modulates the excitability of specific neuronal populations, enhancing or reducing their firing rate depending on the parameters used.

Sessions typically last 20–30 minutes. The most commonly reported sensation is a mild tingling or warmth under the electrodes, which fades quickly. Most patients find it entirely comfortable.

May help with

  • Depression and mood dysregulation
  • Chronic pain modulation
  • Cognitive rehabilitation support
  • Anxiety symptom reduction

Vagus nerve stimulation

tVNS — Transcutaneous
Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation

A small, comfortable clip-style electrode is placed on the ear, delivering gentle electrical stimulation to a branch of the vagus nerve accessible at the skin's surface. This helps regulate activity across brain circuits linked to mood, stress response, and autonomic balance — entirely without needles or implants.

Sessions are quiet and unobtrusive, typically lasting 20–60 minutes, with most patients reporting only a mild tingling at the ear. Because it requires no implanted device, tVNS offers a non-invasive alternative to surgical vagus nerve stimulation.

May help with

  • Anxiety and stress regulation
  • Mood and depressive symptoms
  • Autonomic and heart-rate variability balance
  • Chronic pain and inflammatory conditions

TMS — Transcranial
Magnetic Stimulation

A magnetic coil placed on the scalp delivers brief, focused magnetic pulses to stimulate specific cortical regions. TMS has over 30 years of clinical use and regulatory approval for depression in the United States, Europe, and multiple other jurisdictions.

During a session, patients typically experience a gentle tapping or clicking sensation. Sessions last 20–40 minutes. A standard course involves daily sessions over 4–6 weeks, though accelerated protocols are also available.

TMS is not yet available at ANAVA Clinics — we're working to bring it to our clinics soon. In the meantime, our team can advise whether tDCS or tVNS may suit your needs.

May help with

  • Treatment-resistant depression (evidence strongest)
  • OCD and anxiety disorders
  • Post-stroke motor recovery
  • Tinnitus and chronic pain

TPS — Transcranial
Pulse Stimulation

An innovative ultrasound-based therapy that can reach deeper brain structures than magnetic or electrical stimulation. TPS delivers focused low-intensity ultrasound pulses through the skull, promoting neuroplasticity and potentially influencing circuits previously inaccessible to non-invasive methods.

Delivered via a handheld probe with ultrasound gel, sessions are quiet and comfortable — similar to a physiotherapy ultrasound in sensation. Research into TPS is active and growing, particularly in neurological rehabilitation and cognitive decline.

TPS is an emerging therapy not yet available at ANAVA Clinics — we're working to bring it to our clinics soon. Our team can discuss current research and appropriate expectations during your consultation.

Diagnostics

EEG — measuring the brain
before we treat it

Neuromodulation should be directed by data, not guesswork. We offer EEG — electroencephalography — as part of assessment and follow-up, recording your brain’s electrical activity non-invasively through sensors placed on the scalp. Nothing is injected, nothing is implanted, and the recording itself is entirely painless.

For your clinician

Our platform processes each recording into a detailed report — frequency-band power across delta, theta, alpha and beta, hemispheric asymmetry indices, and artefact-flagged segments. Your clinician uses this to target a protocol to your specific circuits rather than to a diagnostic label.

For you

You receive the same findings in plain language — what was recorded, what it suggests, and what has changed since your last session. Your report is yours to keep and to share with any other doctor involved in your care.

Across your course

A baseline recording before treatment, repeated at intervals, lets your protocol be adjusted against measured change rather than impression alone — and gives an objective record of how your brain has responded over the course.

EEG supports clinical assessment; it is not a standalone diagnostic test and does not by itself diagnose a psychiatric or neurological condition. Findings are always interpreted by a qualified clinician alongside your history and examination. Read more about how your data is handled in our Privacy Policy.
