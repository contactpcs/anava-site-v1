Our story

Built for the patients
who were told there was nothing more.

ANAVA Clinics Pvt Ltd brings together wellness, neuromodulation care, and non-invasive therapy — delivered through clinics and supported end-to-end by a dedicated software platform that connects patients, clinicians, and care seamlessly.

Why ANAVA exists

A clinic designed from
the patient's perspective

The idea for ANAVA grew from a simple, frustrating observation: evidence-based, non-invasive neuromodulation therapies had a growing body of peer-reviewed research behind them — but they remained largely inaccessible to the patients who needed them most. ANAVA was built to close that gap — combining clinical wellness care with a dedicated software platform that keeps every step of the patient journey, from intake to follow-up, connected and transparent.

That platform is designed and built in-house by our technology team in Pune — the same city where our first clinic will open.

The patients we were built for

Most people who come to us are not at the start of their journey. They are somewhere in the middle of it, and tired.

When the medication stops working

Roughly a third of people with depression do not respond adequately to two or more antidepressants taken at an adequate dose for an adequate time. Clinically this is called treatment-resistant depression; in practice it means years of trying one drug, waiting six to eight weeks, tapering off, and starting again — each cycle carrying weight gain, sexual side effects, emotional blunting or fatigue, and each ending in the same place. The problem is not that the patient failed the treatment. It is that a medication acting on brain chemistry system-wide was never targeted at the specific circuits driving their illness.

When pain outlives its cause

People living with chronic pain are frequently told that their scans are clear and that there is nothing more to be done — as though a normal image meant the pain was not real. It is real. Pain circuits become sensitised, and the brain continues generating a pain experience long after the tissue has healed. Meanwhile the conventional path narrows towards long-term analgesics, with the dependency risk that carries, or towards surgical options that address a structure when the problem has moved into the nervous system.

When the alternative is invasive

Where medication fails, what remains on the conventional pathway is often invasive: implanted stimulators, surgical procedures, or anaesthesia-based interventions — each carrying operative risk, recovery time, cost, and the permanence of a decision that is hard to reverse. For many people these are genuinely appropriate. For many others they are simply too much, too soon, and the result is that they choose nothing at all and stay where they are.

And the cost of waiting

Between each of these steps, life continues. Work becomes harder to hold. Relationships strain under an illness nobody can see. Sleep goes, and with it the capacity to cope with everything else. The longer a condition remains untreated, the more entrenched the underlying circuits tend to become — which is precisely why the years spent cycling through options are not neutral waiting time.

Where neuromodulation fits

Non-invasive neuromodulation works at a different point entirely. Rather than flooding the whole system with a drug, or opening the body to place hardware inside it, it applies precisely targeted stimulation from outside the skull to the specific circuits involved in a condition — the prefrontal cortex in depression, the pain-processing networks in chronic pain, the regulatory circuits in anxiety.

Nothing is implanted. There is no anaesthesia, no incision, and no recovery period — patients drive themselves home and return to work the same day. Sessions last twenty to forty minutes. Side effects, where they occur at all, are typically a mild tingling at the scalp or a short-lived headache. And because it is non-invasive, it can be tried without foreclosing anything: it sits alongside the medication and the therapy a patient already has, rather than demanding they abandon what is partly working.

We are careful not to overstate this. Neuromodulation is not a cure, it does not work for everyone, and anyone who promises otherwise should be treated with suspicion. What it offers is a genuine additional option for people who were told they had run out of them — and an honest assessment of whether it is likely to help them before they commit to a course.

Leadership

Meet our co-founders

Vaasudev Chandani

Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer

Vaasudev Chandani leads operations and growth initiatives at ANAVA Clinics, combining product management expertise with a strong background in hardware and embedded systems engineering.

On the hardware side, Vaasudev has engineered advanced medical and assistive devices. He developed an IEEE-published portable Heart Health Monitoring System using ESP32 microcontrollers, AD8232 ECG analog front-ends and MAX30102 sensors to process real-time ECG, SpO2, heart rate and temperature data over Wi-Fi. His hardware work also spans IoT-controlled robotic prosthetic hands, custom DC motor drivers, and 32-bit RISC-V SoC architectures using Verilog and AXI4-Lite protocols.

In his product management capacity, Vaasudev translates complex hardware developments into practical, scalable solutions. By blending data analytics, end-to-end project execution and operational oversight, he drives delivery efficiency, manages vendor partnerships, and ensures long-term strategic growth at ANAVA Clinics.

Our philosophy

Clinically rigorous.
Emotionally intelligent.
Beautifully human.

Evidence first

We only offer wellness and neuromodulation therapies that have meaningful peer-reviewed evidence behind them. We stay current with the research and we never overstate what the evidence supports.

Non-invasive by design

Every therapy and treatment protocol we offer is non-invasive. We will not promise what we cannot deliver — patients who have already been through difficult treatment journeys deserve honest communication, not inflated expectations.

Connected by technology

A dedicated software platform ties every part of the patient journey together — scheduling, treatment protocols, and follow-up — so care stays coordinated across our clinics and our network of specialists.
