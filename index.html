How neurotherapy works

A journey built
around you

No two patients arrive with the same history. Your ANAVA journey begins with understanding yours — and builds from there, carefully and without rush.

01

Patient Data Collection & Assessments

A comprehensive intake process capturing your full history, symptom profile, and prior treatments — alongside clinical assessments to understand your condition precisely.

02

Diagnosis & Personalised Treatment Protocol

Your clinical team reviews all findings and designs a bespoke neurotherapy protocol tailored specifically to your diagnosis and goals — no templates, no shortcuts.

03

In-Clinic Treatment & Home Use

Comfortable, non-invasive sessions in our clinic environment, combined with guided home-use protocols where appropriate — fitting treatment around your life.

04

Your progress is tracked remotely and reviewed at regular in-clinic follow-ups, allowing your protocol to be adjusted in real time as your brain responds.

Our therapies

Evidence-based neurotherapy,
delivered with precision

tDCS and tVNS are available now in our clinics, with TMS and TPS joining our offering soon — each selected and combined based on each patient's individual profile and clinical needs.

tDCS
Brain Stimulation

Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation

tDCS

A gentle, low-level electrical current modulates neuronal excitability in targeted brain regions. Safe, silent, and typically experienced as a mild tingling sensation.

  • Depression & mood disorders
  • Chronic pain modulation
  • Cognitive enhancement in rehabilitation
Learn more about tDCS
tVNS
Vagus Nerve Stimulation

Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation

tVNS

A small electrode placed on the ear gently stimulates the vagus nerve, helping regulate mood, stress response, and autonomic balance — with no implant and no needles.

  • Anxiety and stress regulation
  • Mood and depressive symptoms
  • Autonomic and inflammatory balance
Learn more about tVNS
TMS
Coming Soon

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

TMS

Magnetic pulses stimulate specific cortical regions with established, peer-reviewed protocols. TMS has regulatory approval for depression in multiple countries — we're working to bring it to ANAVA Clinics soon.

  • Treatment-resistant depression
  • Anxiety and OCD protocols
  • Neurological rehabilitation support
Learn more about TMS
TPS
Coming Soon

Transcranial Pulse Stimulation

TPS

An advanced ultrasound-based therapy capable of reaching deep brain structures, particularly promising in neurological rehabilitation and cognitive decline research — we're working to bring it to ANAVA Clinics soon.

  • Deep neurological structures
  • Neurological rehabilitation
  • Cognitive decline support

Our devices

Approved devices.
Not experimental hardware.

Every device used in our clinics is a regulated medical device, selected for its clinical evidence base and its record with health authorities — not assembled in-house and not repurposed consumer hardware. The stimulation systems behind our tDCS protocols hold approvals across several jurisdictions.

Devices are operated by trained clinical staff to protocols set by your treating clinician, with session parameters recorded through our software platform so that every session is traceable.

Treatment duration varies by therapy and by individual. As a general guide:

tDCS sessions typically last 20–30 minutes. A standard course involves daily or near-daily sessions over 2–4 weeks, with some patients using a home-use device between clinic visits for continuity.

tVNS sessions typically last 20–60 minutes, with frequency and course length set according to your individual protocol.

TMS and TPS are coming soon to ANAVA Clinics — once available, a standard TMS course involves 20–30 sessions over 4–6 weeks.

Ultimately, the number of sessions, frequency, and total duration are determined by your diagnosis, how your brain responds, and your doctor's clinical judgement. Your protocol will be designed specifically for you at consultation and adjusted as your treatment progresses.
