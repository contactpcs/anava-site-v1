How neurotherapy works

A journey built
around you

No two patients arrive with the same history. Your ANAVA journey begins with understanding yours — and builds from there, carefully and without rush.

01

Patient Data Collection & Assessments

A comprehensive intake process capturing your full history, symptom profile, and prior treatments — alongside clinical assessments and EEG brain-activity recording, so your condition is understood from measured data and not from description alone.

02

Diagnosis & Personalised Treatment Protocol

Your clinical team reviews all findings — including your EEG report — and designs a bespoke neurotherapy protocol tailored specifically to your diagnosis and goals. No templates, no shortcuts.

03

In-Clinic Treatment & Home Use

Comfortable, non-invasive sessions in our clinic environment, combined with guided home-use protocols where appropriate — fitting treatment around your life.

04

Remote Monitoring & In-Clinic Follow-Up

Your progress is tracked remotely and reviewed at regular in-clinic follow-ups, with EEG re-recorded over the course so change can be seen objectively — allowing your protocol to be adjusted as your brain responds.

Our therapies

delivered with precision

tDCS and tVNS are the therapies we will offer from day one, with TMS and TPS joining our offering as we grow — each selected and combined based on each patient’s individual profile and clinical needs.

tDCS
Brain Stimulation

Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation

tDCS

A gentle, low-level electrical current modulates neuronal excitability in targeted brain regions. Safe, silent, and typically experienced as a mild tingling sensation.

  • Depression & mood disorders
  • Chronic pain modulation
  • Cognitive enhancement in rehabilitation
Learn more about tDCS
tVNS
Vagus Nerve Stimulation

Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation

tVNS

A small electrode placed on the ear gently stimulates the vagus nerve, helping regulate mood, stress response, and autonomic balance — with no implant and no needles.

  • Anxiety and stress regulation
  • Mood and depressive symptoms
  • Autonomic and inflammatory balance
Learn more about tVNS

TMS

  • Neurological rehabilitation support

Corporate mental health

Bringing neurotherapy
into the workplace

High-pressure work does measurable things to the nervous system. ANAVA partners with organisations to bring wellness drives, supervised neuromodulation sessions, and mental health awareness programmes directly to their teams — practical support, delivered on site or at our clinics.

What teams are carrying

  • Sustained stress with no recovery window Deadlines that never fully close, always-on communication, and back-to-back meetings keep the body’s stress response switched on. Without a genuine recovery period, that elevated baseline stops feeling like stress and starts feeling normal.
  • Screen fatigue and fractured attention Long hours across multiple screens, constant context-switching and notification load leave people mentally tired by mid-afternoon — struggling to hold focus on the work that actually matters.
  • Long sitting hours and disrupted sleep Sedentary days, late logins and irregular schedules disturb circadian rhythm and sleep quality. Poor sleep then feeds back into mood, concentration and resilience the following day.
  • Health that gets postponed Early signs — persistent anxiety, low mood, tension headaches, burnout — are noticed and then deferred, because taking time out feels costlier than carrying on. Conditions that respond well early become harder to shift later.
  • Stigma that keeps it quiet Many employees will not raise a mental health concern at work at all. Support that goes unused helps nobody, which is why awareness and access have to be designed together.

How we help

  • On-site wellness drives Structured wellness days at your workplace — stress and sleep screening, individual consultations, and practical guidance your people can use straight away.
  • Supervised neuromodulation sessions Short, non-invasive sessions delivered under clinical supervision. Nothing is implanted, there is no anaesthesia and no downtime — employees return to their day immediately afterwards.
  • Awareness workshops Sessions that explain what stress and burnout actually do to the brain, how to recognise the early signs in yourself and in colleagues, and what genuinely helps — normalising the conversation rather than compartmentalising it.
  • Manager and leadership briefings Focused briefings for the people who set the pace — recognising strain in a team, responding well to disclosure, and building working patterns that protect recovery.
  • A clear onward pathway Where an individual needs more than a workplace programme can offer, they move into a confidential clinical pathway at our clinics — assessed properly, never diagnosed in a corridor.

What neuromodulation offers here

Neuromodulation applies gentle, targeted stimulation to the brain circuits involved in stress regulation, attention and mood. For people under sustained pressure, the aim is straightforward: help an overactive stress response settle, and help the circuits behind focus and recovery work more easily.

Calmer baseline

Supporting the regulatory circuits that bring an overactive stress response back down, so pressure is easier to recover from.

Sharper focus

Targeting the prefrontal circuits behind sustained attention — the capacity that screen fatigue erodes first.

Better rest

Where poor sleep is driven by an over-aroused nervous system, settling that arousal supports the rest that everything else depends on.

Real recovery

Short sessions that fit inside a working day, so looking after your health stops competing with getting your work done.

Corporate programmes are wellness and awareness services. They are not a substitute for individual medical care, and any clinical concern is referred into a properly supervised assessment. Outcomes vary between individuals, and we will always be candid with your organisation about what the evidence does and does not support.

Our devices

Purpose-built devices.
Not experimental hardware.

We use a combination of clinically approved, medical-grade and wellness-grade devices, matched to the treatment being delivered. Each one is selected for its evidence base and its manufacturing standards — not assembled in-house and not repurposed consumer hardware. The stimulation systems behind our clinical tDCS protocols hold regulatory approvals across several jurisdictions; devices used for wellness protocols are selected to the appropriate standard for that use.

Devices are operated by trained clinical staff to protocols set by your treating clinician, with session parameters recorded through our software platform so that every session is traceable.

Treatment duration varies by therapy and by individual. As a general guide:

tDCS sessions typically last 20–30 minutes. A standard course involves daily or near-daily sessions, with benefit generally building gradually and peaking at around six weeks. Some patients use a home-use device between clinic visits for continuity.

tVNS sessions typically last 20–60 minutes, with frequency and course length set according to your individual protocol.

TMS and TPS are coming soon to ANAVA Clinics — once available, a standard TMS course involves 20–30 sessions over 4–6 weeks.

Ultimately, the number of sessions, frequency, and total duration are determined by your diagnosis, how your brain responds, and your doctor's clinical judgement. Your protocol will be designed specifically for you at consultation and adjusted as your treatment progresses.
